## Observed weather and nearby stations (IEM ASOS)

We downloaded routine and special METARs from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet for LGA, JFK, EWR, TEB and Central Park (NYC) for 2020 to 2026 and for HPN, ISP, FRG, CDW and SMQ for 2021 to 2026: 65 station-year files in 73 HTTP requests, counting retries after rate limiting (data/asos_sources.csv).
At each cutoff a report counts only if its valid time is strictly before the cutoff minus a 10-minute delivery allowance and at most 90 minutes older than the cutoff. Routine reports at these sites are taken at :51 to :56, so this rule drops the report from the hour just before the cutoff, and the newest usable routine report is 64 to 69 minutes old.
LGA's own weather (wind u and v, gust, altimeter and its 3 h change, dew point depression, sky cover, ceiling, 3 h precipitation) was added to CURRENT as (a). Temperature relative to LGA for the six new sites and sky cover for all nine other sites were added on top as (b). We fit the same censored Gaussian on the same 1,040 (09:00, 12:00) and 1,038 (15:00) complete 2021-2023 rows, filled missing values with the training median and chose L2 on 2024.
Neither helps. Test log loss for (a) is 0.2003 / 0.1602 / 0.0738 and for (b) 0.2028 / 0.1617 / 0.0733 at 09:00 / 12:00 / 15:00, against 0.1995 / 0.1572 / 0.0722 for CURRENT on the same 359 days. Every paired 7-day block bootstrap interval for log loss includes zero, for example (a) at 12:00 changes log loss by +0.0031 (95% interval -0.0008 to +0.0069). Accuracy at 12:00 falls by 1.1 points for (a) and 1.7 points for (b), and both of those intervals exclude zero.
Validation agrees. On 2024, (a) and (b) are worse than CURRENT at every cutoff (0.2117 / 0.1739 / 0.1013 and 0.2134 / 0.1738 / 0.1067 against 0.2111 / 0.1718 / 0.0981).
In the ablation no single group added to CURRENT improves test log loss by more than 0.0006 at any cutoff. Validation picks CURRENT plus nearby sky cover at 09:00 and 12:00 and CURRENT plus LGA wind at 15:00, with margins of 0.0010, 0.0026 and 0.0001. On test these change log loss by +0.0002, +0.0024 and -0.0001, and every interval includes zero. Margins this small are within selection noise across 18 candidates, so we keep CURRENT, the simpler model.
Removing any one group from (b) never beats CURRENT. The only interval that excludes zero is a loss: (b) without pressure at 09:00, +0.0073 (+0.0009 to +0.0138).
The 10-minute rule is not what hides a gain. With a 5-minute allowance, which keeps the :51 report as the paper's own station features do, (a) scores 0.1993 / 0.1596 / 0.0740 and (b) 0.2055 / 0.1526 / 0.0733. That 12:00 value for (b) is the only sizeable test improvement, but it is the worst of these systems on validation (0.1766), so it would not be chosen.
The features are not empty, they are redundant. In an observations-only model without HRRR, LAMP or NBM, adding (a) improves 09:00 test log loss from 0.3765 to 0.3576 (-0.0189, interval -0.0292 to -0.0087). It changes nothing reliable at 12:00 or 15:00.
Our reading is that at lead times of 2 to 15 hours, the guidance already carries what the latest METAR says about wind, cloud and moisture. Own-station weather pays off mainly in minute-scale nowcasting, where no forecast is available. Results are in results/observed_weather/.
