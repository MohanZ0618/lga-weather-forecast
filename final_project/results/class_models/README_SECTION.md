## Course ensembles and a neural network

`python final_project/class_models.py` (about one minute, offline, two threads; checks in `test_class_models.py`) tests the Lecture 11 and 13 models on CURRENT's inputs, its 1,040 training days (2021 to 2023) and the same 359 test days, with every choice made on 2024. Differences are paired by date against CURRENT's saved test probabilities with 7-day block bootstrap intervals; our refit of CURRENT reproduces them to within 0.0001 log loss at every cutoff.

**Bagging.** Averaging 50 censored Gaussians fit to bootstrap resamples of training weeks leaves 2024 log loss unchanged (0.2113, 0.1710, 0.0981 against 0.2111, 0.1712, 0.0981 at 09:00, 12:00, 15:00) and lowers test log loss by only 0.0013, 0.0015 and 0.0009; only the 15:00 interval, [-0.0019, -0.0001], excludes zero, and validation would not choose it.
The bias-variance view explains why: across bootstrap members the predicted mean warming moves by 0.35 to 0.41 F while the forecast spread is 1.6 to 1.7 F, so estimation variance is about 4 to 7% of the predictive variance and there is little for averaging to remove. Bagging pays off for high-variance learners such as deep trees, which is what a random forest exploits.

**Neural network.** A one-hidden-layer tanh network outputs the mean and log spread of the same censored Gaussian and is trained by minibatch Adam on the censored likelihood, with hand-written backpropagation (checked against finite differences), L2 weight decay and early stopping on 2024. Validation chose width 4, 4 and 16 from 24 settings (a linear input-to-output path at 09:00 and 15:00) and stopped after 8 to 88 epochs.
Averaging five seeds lowered 2024 log loss in all 72 setting and cutoff cells (by 0.0003 to 0.013), the variance reduction that bagging could not deliver for the linear model. The network is worse than CURRENT on 2024 at 09:00 and 12:00 (0.2209, 0.1738) and better at 15:00 (0.0917); on test it scores 0.1949, 0.1689 and 0.0748, and every paired interval includes zero (for example +0.0026 [-0.0037, +0.0090] at 15:00).

**Stacking.** The paper's classifier families refit on the same inputs select an average of a random forest and XGBoost (boosting) at every cutoff and lose clearly (test 0.2272, 0.2170, 0.0901); 2024 gives them zero weight in every pool with CURRENT, and the equal-weight pool is worse on both splits. Pooling CURRENT with the network, 2024 puts weight 1.0, 0.6 and 0.0 on CURRENT.

**What validation selects.** With fitted pools scored by 2-fold cross-fitting inside 2024, validation keeps CURRENT at 09:00, picks the equal CURRENT and network pool at 12:00 and the network alone at 15:00. On test those picks score 0.1995, 0.1600 and 0.0748 against CURRENT's 0.1995, 0.1591 and 0.0722 (+0.0009 [-0.0086, +0.0097] and +0.0026 [-0.0037, +0.0090]), so nothing in this lane beats CURRENT.
The 2024 gains behind both switches (-0.0014 and -0.0064) had intervals that included zero, and the network's 2024 score is optimistic because its early stopping and settings used the same days. A rule that switches only when the 2024 interval excludes zero keeps CURRENT at every cutoff, so CURRENT stays the system.
